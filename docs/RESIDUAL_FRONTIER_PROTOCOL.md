## Model and optimization

The only trainable quantities are 21 corrections to the seven original weights in opening/middle/endgame order. Original features, normalization, boundaries at 28/52 occupied, terminal utility, passes and alpha-beta remain unchanged. All corrections start at exactly zero. Deployment compiles `B weight + correction` once and calls the original fast evaluator with those weights; there is no second feature scan. A zero correction has exactly identical scores, moves, nodes and evaluator calls. Nonzero compiled and explicitly added residual arithmetic is compared with tolerance `1e-9 × max(1, |a|, |b|)`; summation rounding can differ. Exact terminal utilities are never fitted.

For preferred child x over y, retain `base = B(x,p) - B(y,p)` and the difference of their 21-dimensional phase-block feature vectors. Each child uses its own board's phase. A terminal child's vector is zero and its offset is fixed production terminal utility. Features use parent player p, including when the next player must pass.

For each informative independent trajectory, divide each raw preference weight by the sum of all that trajectory's preference weights, across both parents. Average those unit-mass trajectory losses. A source without supported preferences contributes no artificial target; report its absence/yield. Minimize this source-mean logistic preference loss with temperature **10 B units**, plus **λ times the sum of squared corrections in original weight units**. λ is one of **0.0001, 0.001, 0.01**. Bounds are **±25 original weight units**. Edax discs do not enter the B-score margin or temperature.

The numerical method is deterministic projected gradient with Armijo backtracking. The analytical gradient accumulates the feature **difference** before preconditioning. Compute training-only, source-weighted uncentered RMS pair differences, floored at .01. The diagonal preconditioner is `2λ + RMS²/(4×10²)`. It changes update directions only: there is no centering, model rescaling, penalty rescaling, development normalization or holdout preprocessing. Step growth, shrinkage, tolerance and iteration cap are fixed in the executable protocol. Central finite differences check all 21 coordinates; extreme margins check numerical stability. Checkpoints bind optimizer state to corpus checksum, λ and moments.

Select on development unregularized source-mean preference loss, including unchanged B. Ties within 1e-12 favor smaller correction norm. Freeze one selected model and checksum. Learning curves at 100/200/400/800/1,600 independent training sources use the final development-selected λ; they cannot select a new finalist. If B wins selection, the zero control remains the candidate and cannot pass a 5% improvement gate.

## Sources, separation and witnesses

Reserve **24 pilot, 1,600 training, 400 development, 400 locked assessment and 256 game-opening trajectories**. The fixed 128-root transfer subset comes from the 400 assessment roots: eight per stratum/policy/source-depth cell. Assessment evaluates new root choices at both depths four and six. Four occupied strata are 12–19, 20–35, 36–47 and 48–55. Within each scientific positional corpus, the two source policies B1/12% and B4/2%, and search depths four/six, have equal counts: 100 training or 25 development/assessment per cell. Parity and turn counts differ by at most one within each cell. They are balanced marginals, not an artificial independent parity/turn cross: before a pass, placement parity determines whose turn it is. Source trajectories start with random moves before occupied 12 and then follow the declared epsilon-greedy B policy to termination. All seeds and attempts are deterministic. Interleaved cell order gives balanced learning-curve prefixes. Game openings alternate occupied 12/13 and policy, with both turns.

Admission checks the complete post-12 source trajectory, root alternatives, continuation boards, frontier parents and **every queried frontier child**, under both turn/color orientations and all D4 symmetries. Full SHA-256 canonical keys are stored in a compact sorted binary index; no hash truncation. The historical index includes prior campaigns, qualifications, teacher-audit receipts, disclosed fixtures, query PV boards and prior residual reservations. Missing historical inputs stop admission. Accepted trajectories cannot overlap any other reserved source at or beyond 12 occupied, even within a role. Common randomized prefixes below 12 remain disclosed dependencies. Rejected attempts and unused generated task receipts remain local. Complete source trajectories and frontier witnesses are kept per root. All scientific roles and the transfer subset are jointly frozen **before any scientific training label**; engineering labels are separate.

Each source root's legal alternatives get independent full windows in the unchanged B search. Sort exact depth-limited scores by descending value then row-major move. For the first two alternatives, independently re-search every required continuation node and every legal move there with a fresh search context at the correct remaining placement depth. Never trust a TT best move or a cutoff bound. Verify each backed-up value against the preceding alternative's value with alternating perspective. Passes preserve remaining placements, and terminal detection precedes cutoff evaluation. A forced-pass source root is not admitted (at least two legal moves required); passes within continuations are retained.

At remaining depth one, independently enumerate child boards with the sentinel-board rules oracle. Check each child's cutoff score against the full-window depth-one row. Save all rows, players, occupied counts, phases, passes and original-alternative provenance. Also record whether the selected endpoint was directly observed by the original alternative search's evaluator callback. A tied optimum can reconstruct another equally valid endpoint; the independently verified minimax witness and final static equality establish its horizon role. Do not assert that every teacher-labeled sibling was visited by alpha-beta or causally determined the original root result. Early terminal lines are recorded without replacement. Deduplicate canonical frontier parents inside a source; cross-source admission prevents duplicated frontier children.
