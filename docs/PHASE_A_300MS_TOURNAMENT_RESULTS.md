## Where B still succeeds

B wins 179 individual games and 13 complete two-color sweeps. The
[descriptive diagnostics](data/phase-a-300ms/diagnostics.json) preserve three
large B wins with full searched moves, depths and actual corner captures.
Examples use zero-based game/ply IDs and standard a1–h8 coordinates:

- Game 486, family 144, P Black: actual opening continuation `P f4, B g8,
  P f5, B h8`; B takes h8 at ply 3 (occupied 15, depth 11), later all four
  corners, and wins by 64 raw discs. The independent replay verifies the wipeout.
- Game 267, family 207, P Black: `P g6, B e8, P c5, B h6, P c7, B h8`;
  B takes h8 at ply 5 (occupied 18, depth 10) and wins by 54.
- Game 453, family 103, P White: B's later corner captures at plies 38,
  42 and 44 accompany a 56-disc win.

These are selected illustrations of corner-pressure positions in which P can
still lose heavily. They do **not** prove that the preceding P move was the
losing mistake, that the corner could have been prevented, or that a particular
static feature caused a deeper minimax score. Establishing that requires matched
counterfactual continuations or exact regret. The current sample does not identify
a reliable new position class in which B is generally superior. Retaining these
real games for a causal strategy coach is more useful than inventing an unsupported
feature attribution or launching more post hoc confirmation tests.
