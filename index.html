Full game rules

Black moves first from the four central discs. Place a disc on an empty square so that at least one straight line of opposing discs is bracketed between your new disc and an existing disc of your color. Lines can run horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, without gaps. Flip every bracketed opposing disc in all eight directions.

You must make a legal move when one exists. If you have none, you must pass and your opponent moves again. The game ends when neither player has a legal move, even if empty squares remain; a full board or elimination of one color also ends play. The player with more discs wins; equal counts are a draw. Empty squares do not count toward either score.

What makes a move good?

Consider mobility, corners, vulnerable corner-adjacent squares, and your opponent's replies—not just the number of discs gained immediately.

USING THE COACH

Compare choices, then test your ideas

Select any move in the table and choose another in the comparison menu. Compare their scores and verified continuations, including the opponent’s replies.

Positive scores favor the side to move. Heuristic estimates depend on search depth; solved results reach the end of the game. Immediate feature figures describe the resulting position, not the deeper search score.

Replay earlier positions with the slider, undo a turn to try another plan, or review your decisions after a completed game.
